About Habesha Games

Ethiopian-themed games you can play right in your browser — no downloads, no sign-up.

Habesha Games is a free online arcade celebrating Ethiopian culture through play. Race a blue taxi through the streets of Addis Ababa, stack injera high on a woven sefed, run with a jebena coffee pot in Buna Run, test your knowledge of worship songs, or learn Amharic words with Copy Me.

Who it is for

Everyone — the Ethiopian diaspora missing home, families wanting fun games with a cultural twist, Amharic learners, and anyone who loves a good arcade game. The games are in English and Amharic, so they are a playful way to stay connected to the language too.

How it works

  • Pick a game and tap to play — it opens right on the page.
  • Everything runs in your browser. Nothing to install, no account needed.
  • New games join the library as they are ready.

How the games stay free

Habesha Games is supported by advertising. Ads keep every game free for every player. See our Privacy Policy for details on how ads work here.
