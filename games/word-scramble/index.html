How to play

Tap "Start the game" and the game shows you one word at a time — its letters all mixed up. Tap the letters in the right order to build the word. Tap a placed letter to take it back and try a different order. You have 15 seconds for each word: finish in time and the game shows you the word with its English meaning. If the clock runs out, the game shows you the answer and you move on to the next word. There are no scores here — just play!

Play again and the words come in a fresh order, so the game never plays the same way twice. It is a lovely game for the whole family: every word is a real challenge — 5 letters or more — and everyone races on the longest one at the end.

Tips to play well

  • Say the letters out loud. Hearing the sounds helps the word click — Amharic letters are sounds, so sounding them out is half the game.
  • Look for a familiar start. Many Amharic words start with አ, በ, መ or ሰ — if one of those is in the mix, try it first.
  • Wrong order? Just take it back. Tap a placed letter to return it and try again — the clock keeps running, so keep moving.
  • Play it as a race. With a group, the first person to shout the word wins the round. First to 5 wins the game.

Why it's fun

ቃላት አደራድር — unscrambling words — is a perfect fit for Amharic: every fidel is a full sound, so putting a word back together feels like putting sounds back together. Every word is at least 5 letters, so every round is a real challenge — and the longest word — እግዚአብሔር — waits at the very end as the finale.
