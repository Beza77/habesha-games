Habesha Games ("we", "our") is a free collection of Ethiopian-themed browser games at habeshagames.com. This policy explains what information is collected when you visit and how it is used.

Advertising

We use Google AdSense to show ads on this site. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads based on your visits to this site and other sites on the internet.

How we use information

Information collected is used to operate and improve the games, understand how the site is used, keep it secure, and show advertising that keeps the games free.

Data sharing

We do not sell your personal information. Data may be shared with service providers that help us run the site (such as hosting and advertising partners), as described above.

Children

Habesha Games is a general-audience website. We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children. If you believe a child has provided us personal information, contact us and we will remove it.

Your choices

You can control cookies through your browser settings, and opt out of personalized ads using the links above. Note that blocking cookies may affect how the games work.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date above will reflect the latest version.
